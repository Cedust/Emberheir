# Story-Texte – Entwurf v1

Stand: 30.09.2026. Alle Spieltexte auf Englisch (Projektkonvention). Grundlage: `story-theme-v1.md`.
Ton: hell, mit Humor, wenig Text. Keine Zwischensequenzen: alles erscheint als kurzer Text am Hearthfire, in der Act-Vorschau oder als Sprechblase einer Persona.
Markierung: 💡 Vorschlag. Alles hier ist ein erster Entwurf.

---

## 1. Intro 💡

Erscheint beim ersten Start, erzählt von Old Nan am Hearthfire. Fünf kurze Tafeln, jede per Klick weiter.

> **1.** Every world has seasons. This one has harvests.
>
> **2.** Long ago, the Ashen Harvester fell from the sky and liked what it saw: rich soil, busy people, and plenty to burn. So it let the land grow. And when it was ripe, it burned it all and feasted on the flames.
>
> **3.** The ash settles. The world grows back, a little stronger every time. More monsters. More treasure. More to harvest. It has never once skipped a harvest.
>
> **4.** But the fire always leaves something behind. A caravan that carries the last true flame, the Hearthfire. And an Heir, bound to the ash, who cannot die while the Harvester still burns.
>
> **5.** That's you, dear. Up you get. The Harvester is waiting, and the soup won't cook itself.
> — *Old Nan*

## 2. Act-Beschreibungen 💡

Erscheinen in der Act-Vorschau (Scout) und beim Betreten eines Acts. Je eine Zeile Titel, zwei bis drei Sätze Text, dazu der Hinweis auf den Schwerpunkt.

**Act 1 · Ashen Fields**
> Grey fields, black trees, and the smell of last year's apocalypse. The scavengers out here fight dirty, and the biggest of them has dug himself a pit and called it a kingdom.
> *Warden: Gorrak, the Pit Brute. Watch for his Slam.*

**Act 2 · Rotwood**
> Where the ash fell thickest, the forest grew back wrong. Everything here is alive, hungry, and slightly damp. Bring something against poison, and don't eat the mushrooms.
> *Warden: Mother of Rot. Heals herself, poisons you.*

**Act 3 · Ember Wastes**
> The Harvester's fire never fully went out here. The ground glows, the air shimmers, and the locals are literally on fire. Pack something fireproof. Preferably yourself.
> *Warden: Cinder Tyrant. Burns hotter when cornered.*

**Act 4 · Frost Peaks**
> High above the ash, the cold has been waiting a long time for someone to freeze. Up here even your fighting spirit turns sluggish. Keep moving, keep warm.
> *Warden: Rime Warden. Hides behind ice. Break it.*

**Act 5 · Storm Spires**
> Towers of rock that attract lightning the way the Heir attracts trouble. Hit fast, or hit hard, but never hit the wrong moment.
> *Warden: Storm Herald. Strike carelessly, and the storm strikes back.*

**Act 6 · Void Rift**
> Here the world burned so often that it simply stopped. What lives in the gap is patient, and it gets stronger the longer you stay. So don't stay.
> *Warden: Voidborn Maw. Corruption that only grows.*

**Act 7 · Emberfall**
> Where the star came down, the ground never stopped glowing. Every harvest ends here, carried in on the wind as sparks. Everything the Harvester has taken is here, and so is the Harvester. It has been expecting you. It always is.
> *The Ashen Harvester.*

## 3. Kennenlern-Szenen 💡

Jeweils drei bis fünf Zeilen, einmalig beim ersten Beitritt (danach erinnern sich die Personas über Prestiges hinweg).

### Start-Camp (vor Act 1): Old Nan, Thoric, Marisha
> **Old Nan:** Awake again? Good. You were snoring through the end of the world.
> **Thoric:** Wagon's burned. Roof's burned. My good hammer's burned. Everything's burned.
> **Marisha:** Not my stock! Fireproof crates, darling. First customer gets a discount. Second customer pays for the first.
> **Old Nan:** Go on then, Heir. The fields won't clear themselves.

### Nach Act 1: Liora (Mystic)
*Liora sitzt in Gorraks Grube, an eine Kette gebunden.*
> **Liora:** I knew someone would come. I saw it in the stars.
> **Liora:** ...I also saw Gorrak living to a hundred, so perhaps don't trust the stars too much.
> **Liora:** Take me with you. I can read what's hidden in your gear. Mostly correctly.

### Nach Act 1: Kaelen (Trainer)
*Kaelen kommt in voller Rüstung angerannt, als alles vorbei ist.*
> **Kaelen:** Stand back, villain! I, Kaelen of the Last Vigil, will... oh. He's dead.
> **Kaelen:** Late again. Last time I was late, my entire order marched off without me. Against the Harvester. None came back.
> **Kaelen:** You fight like someone who taught themselves by the campfire. Admirable. Also painful to watch.
> **Kaelen:** Come to me from now on. I'll show you paths you never knew you had. And should you stray from the right one, I'll walk you back.

### Nach Act 2: Eldrin (Runesmith)
*Eldrin steckt bis zur Brust in einer Ranke fest.*
> **Eldrin:** Don't mind me. I was reading. The wall was reading me back, apparently.
> **Eldrin:** These ruins are covered in runes, you know. Old ones. Combine three and you get a better one! Isn't that marvellous? Nobody ever thinks it's marvellous.
> **Eldrin:** If you cut me free, I'll teach you everything. Please cut me free. It's starting to digest.

### Nach Act 2: Nyssa (Scout)
*Nyssa steht plötzlich mitten im Camp.*
> **Thoric:** How long has she been there?
> **Nyssa:** Since Rotwood. You people make a lot of noise for a secret caravan.
> **Nyssa:** I've seen what's ahead. You won't like it. But you'll like it less if you don't know. I'll scout, you fight. Fair?

## 4. Persona-Sprüche 💡

Zufällige Zeilen beim Anklicken im Camp. Pro Persona vier Beispiele, später je 10–20.

**Old Nan (Hearthkeeper)**
- "Sit, dear. The fire's warm and the world's still standing. For now."
- "My name? Oh, I had one. Lovely one, I think. Must have left it somewhere."
- "I've seen a lot of Heirs. You have the nicest boots so far."
- "The Harvester's a farmer. Farmers get weeds. Be a weed, dear."

**Thoric (Blacksmith)**
- "Heard the Harvester's coming for the harvest again. Figures. Right when I'd fixed the roof."
- "Saved one thing from my forge. The anvil. Couldn't lift anything else."
- "Bring me Ascension Shards and I'll make that blade remember it's a blade."
- "Salvage it? Gladly. Some of this is an insult to metal."

**Marisha (Merchant)**
- "Buy now! Everything comes back stronger after the apocalypse. Especially my prices."
- "Where do I get my goods? Darling, a merchant never tells. Also, I don't remember."
- "Three apocalypses and I've never had a single refund. Nobody's alive to ask."
- "Gamble? Of course. The house always wins. I am the house."

**Liora (Mystic)**
- "Your future is bright. Or on fire. It's hard to tell with this lot."
- "Ten thousand years of farming this world, and he still hasn't learned to rotate his crops."
- "I can reroll that affix. Whether the new one is better, the stars won't say."
- "I once predicted my own birthday. Missed by a week."

**Kaelen (Trainer)**
- "Again! And this time with feeling!"
- "A wrong build is not a failure. It is a lesson. An expensive lesson."
- "I will be on time for the final battle. I have set three reminders."
- "Respec is no shame. Staying wrong is."

**Eldrin (Runesmith)**
- "Did you know there are runes older than the Harvester? Nobody asks about those."
- "Three small runes make one bigger rune. It's basically poetry. With rocks."
- "The Codex remembers every Runeword you find. Unlike some people. Unlike Nan."
- "Careful with that one. It's ancient, powerful, and slightly sticky."

**Nyssa (Scout)**
- "Next area is worse. It's always worse."
- "Want to go back to an old camp? Fine. I know the way. I always know the way."
- "I counted the enemies ahead. Then I stopped counting. Bad sign."
- "You fight loud. I like that. Makes it easy to find you."

## 5. Tod, Retreat und Prestige 💡

**Tod** (Old Nan, zufällig):
- "Back so soon? Sit. Eat something. Try again."
- "That's the fourth time today. I'm not counting. Thoric is."
- "The ash spat you back out. It does that. It likes you."

**Retreat** (Old Nan):
- "Sensible. Dead heroes don't farm."

**Stash beim ersten Halt repariert** (Thoric, einmal pro Prestige):
- "Patched the wagon. Again. Don't overload it. Again."

**Sieg über den Harvester** (Durchgang 1 bis 9, letzte Worte, bevor er zerbirst):
> "You didn't kill me, little Heir. You *harvested* me."

**Prestige** (Old Nan, nach dem Sieg über den Harvester):
> "It's down. And there it goes, the whole world, up in smoke. Don't worry, dear, it'll grow back. It always does.
> Hold on to what matters. The fire can't take what you've sealed.
> Another harvest, another Heir. Sit down, child. The fire's warm."

**Ende** (nach dem Finale The Last Ember, die letzte Flamme ist im Hearthfire):
> "No smoke. No ash. Just... quiet. Well. I suppose I'll need to remember my name after all."
> — *Old Nan*
