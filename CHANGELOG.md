# Changelog

Each version gets a section here. A push to `main` that raises the version in the root
`package.json` tags it (`v<version>`) and publishes this section as its GitHub Release
(`.github/workflows/release.yml`).

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-09

The first release of **Emberheir: Rise from the Ashes**, a browser auto-battler with rogue-lite
runs and prestige.

- **Three classes, nine weapons:** Cinderguard, Ashstrider and Emberseer, each with their own
  Heirloom weapon, three Heirloom paths, Masteries, Class Trait and a big Ash Tree.
- **Seven acts and a finale:** an act map with Hunts, Elites, Gatekeepers, Waymarks and a boss
  per act, the Ashen Harvester, The Last Ember and the Wildfire tiers I–X for the end game.
- **Loot at the core:** Normal to Legendary items, Uniques, Legendary Powers, Runes and Runewords,
  crafting in the Camp (Reforge, Temper, Upgrade), Marisha's gamble and a shared Supply Wagon.
- **Battle Plan:** your own rotation and reactions, Heat Forms, Draughts and Boons in the Wildfire.
- **Camp and story:** painted camp personas met on the road, Old Nan's story intro, First Steps
  tips, Compendium, Bounties and Eldrin's Cache, Collections and Lineage.
- **Painted look and sound:** painted characters, enemies, arenas, icons and UI, curated battle
  effects, music and battle sounds; light and dark parchment themes, tablet touch controls.
- **Saves:** sealed save games, backup export and import, optional cloud saves with Google or
  GitHub sign-in. The save format starts fresh with this release: saves of the test builds no
  longer load.
